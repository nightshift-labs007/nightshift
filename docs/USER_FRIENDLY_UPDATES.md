# What's new on Nightshift: an easier site to find your way around

People told us that when they landed on nightshift.buzz they weren't sure what to do next. So we made the site
explain itself, show who we build with, and give every visitor something of their own: a profile, points and a place
on the leaderboard.

Here's everything that changed.

---

## 1. A short guide when you first arrive

The first time you open the homepage, a small window walks you through Nightshift in five quick steps:

1. **What Nightshift is.** Stock markets close at 4pm, but tokenized ETFs trade all night. Nightshift keeps pricing
   them, so you can lend and borrow against them safely.
2. **How to read the prices.** What the fair value, the % move and "CONF" (how confident the engine is) mean.
3. **How to try borrowing.** Use a practice account with no wallet, or connect a wallet and grab free demo tokens.
4. **How to stay protected.** Switch on the Guardian, which steps in before a loan gets liquidated.
5. **How to check our work.** Where to find the proofs and the public prediction record.

Every step has a button that takes you straight there. The App page has its own shorter guide: borrowing in four
moves, and what the "health factor" means.

**Already know your way around?** Click *Skip*, and the guide won't show again on that browser.

**Want to see it again?** A **Guide** button sits in the bottom-left corner of every page.

---

## 2. A menu that explains every page

Click the **menu button next to the Nightshift logo**, or on a phone **swipe in from the left edge**. A panel slides
out showing:

- **Every page, with one line on what it does.** For example, "Oracle: live overnight prices for SPY, QQQ, gold and
  bitcoin ETFs".
- **Every protocol we work with**, with its logo, how Nightshift uses it, and a link to the page where you can see it
  in action.

On phones and smaller screens, this panel is the main menu, so nothing gets squeezed or cut off.

---

## 3. "Built with": the protocols behind Nightshift

A new section on the homepage shows the five projects Nightshift is built on. Each card shows the logo and the X
account, says what we use it for and why that matters to you, and links to live proof.

| Protocol | What it does for Nightshift |
|---|---|
| **Harmonic** | Its AI, Aristotle, machine-checked 24 math proofs of our safety rules. For example, "a healthy loan can never be liquidated" is proven, not just promised. |
| **Morpho** | Our lending rules follow Morpho's, and our price feeds plug straight into Morpho markets on Base. |
| **Algorand** | Every morning's price prediction is locked on Algorand *before* the market opens, so our track record can't be edited afterwards. |
| **Sentora** | Shows a live yield vault where idle stablecoins could earn a protected rate. Deposits happen on Sentora's own site. |
| **ZKProof** | Our "Cloak" feature uses the kind of zero-knowledge proof ZKProof's standards cover. It proves a loan is safe without revealing its size. |

Logos belong to their owners. They show technical integrations, not endorsements or partnerships.

## 4. The leaderboard

A new **Leaderboard** page (in the top menu) ranks everyone who has tried Nightshift.

**How to get on it:** connect your wallet and click **Join**. You'll sign a free message: no transaction, no gas, no
cost. Shortly after you connect, a small card asks whether you'd like to join, but only once per wallet.

**How points work:**

| What you do | Points |
|---|---|
| Join the leaderboard | +10 |
| Deposit an ETF token in the Vault | +20 for each token |
| Have an open loan | +30 |
| Switch on the Guardian | +25 |

Ties go to whoever has more collateral, then to whoever joined first.

**Why you can trust it:** we don't keep the list. Joins are recorded on Algorand and loans live on Base, both public
blockchains, so nobody can quietly change the rankings, including us.

*Demo only: points have no money value.*

---

## 5. Your own profile

A new **Profile** page lets every wallet build a profile. Open it from the wallet menu with **Your profile & points**,
or from the side panel.

**What you can add:** a display name, email, X handle, Telegram, website and a short bio.

**What you'll see:**
- your points and your rank
- a checklist of what you've done, and what earns more, with a button for each step
- your collateral in the Vault

**Your wallet is your login.** Sign one free message (no password, no transaction), and you stay signed in for 7 days
on that browser.

**Your privacy:**
- Your **email, Telegram and website are never shown publicly**.
- Your **name and X handle appear on the leaderboard only if you tick the box**.
- You can **edit or delete your profile at any time**. Profiles are kept in a private database, not on a blockchain,
  so deleting really removes them.

## 6. Smaller things that help

- **Honest wording.** Each automated X post was checked against what its proof actually says, and three that
  promised too much were rewritten.
- **Fair limits.** Limits on the free demo-token faucet and on leaderboard joins now hold steady, so nobody can spam
  them.
- **Works on phones.** Every new feature was checked at phone size, desktop size and the sizes in between.

---

## In one sentence

New visitors now get a guided tour, a clear map of every page and partner, and a reason to come back: a profile,
points and a public leaderboard they can't be cheated on.
