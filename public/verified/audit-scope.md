# Nightshift: audit scope and threat model

Prepared for an independent smart-contract audit. Nightshift is currently an **unaudited demo on Base mainnet using
demo tokens with no value**. The audit is the gate before any real collateral or loan asset is used.

## 3. Roles and trust assumptions

| Role | Holder | Powers | Risk if compromised |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oracle `owner` | one EOA (deployer) | `forceSetPrices` (no move limit), `setUpdater`, `setMaxMoveBps`, `transferOwnership` | Can set any price, so it can liquidate every position or allow over-borrowing. **Highest-trust key.** |
| Oracle updater | keeper EOA | `setPrices`, limited to ±`maxMoveBps` (15%) per update | Can walk a price 15% per update, so it can drift arbitrarily over several updates. |
| Vault `owner` | same EOA | `setMarket` (enable or disable, change LLTV) | Raising LLTV or disabling a market can make positions liquidatable or stuck. |
| Anyone | | `protect` (only within the borrower's own rule), `liquidate` (only HF < 1) | |

There is no timelock, multisig or pause. That is deliberate for the demo. For production, see section 7.

## 4. Invariants to check

These are machine-proven in Lean 4 over a model of the contracts: `formal/`, public at
`github.com/nightshift-labs007/nightshift-formal`, 24 properties. The `formal/MAPPING.md` file maps each one to the
Solidity. The audit should confirm that the Solidity matches the model.

- **V1:** a healthy position (HF ≥ 1) cannot be liquidated.
- **V2, V3, V4:** HF rises with price and with collateral, and falls with debt.
- **V5:** integer rounding favours the protocol: HF ≥ 1 on-chain implies HF ≥ 1 in exact math.
- **V6:** liquidation bonus ≤ 15%.
- **V7:** borrowing within `borrowLimit` leaves HF ≥ 1 at that price.
- **G1–G5:** the Guardian sale is ≥ 0 and ≤ collateral, it reaches the target in exact math and is minimal, it never sells when that can't help, and it only acts on savable positions.
- **C1–C4:** Cloak circuit bounds, with no field wrap-around and conservative rounding.

Also tested:
- **Fuzz:** `scripts/fuzz-guardian.mjs` ran 34,708 random price moves with 0 invariant violations; results in `public/verified/guardian-fuzz.json`.
- **Unit and adversarial tests:** `scripts/test-phase2-contracts.mjs`, 19 tests, including re-entry by a malicious callback token.

## 5. Known issues and design decisions (please confirm or challenge)

1. **Bad debt is not socialized.** If `seized` is capped at the position's collateral, the remaining debt stays on the empty position. Lenders aren't written down, because the Vault itself is the lender in the demo.
2. **The Guardian sells to the Vault.** `protect` moves collateral from the borrower to the Vault at 0.5% below the Oracle price, with no DEX swap. The Vault therefore holds collateral inventory and price risk.
3. **The Morpho adapter ignores confidence.** Morpho's `IOracle` has no confidence field. The adapter enforces only `maxAge` (1 day). By contrast, the Vault decays confidence on-chain by 1 point per hour and refuses prices older than 2 days.
4. **Liquidation reads only HF < 1.** There's no partial-liquidation close factor; a liquidator may repay the whole debt.
5. **`forceSetPrices` bypasses the move limit** by design, for genuine crashes. Combined with a single-EOA owner, it's the main centralization point.
6. **Slither** (`public/verified/security/slither.json`): 2 real Medium findings fixed (stale price in the adapter; state changed after transfers). The rest are accepted or false positives, each explained in that file.
7. **Timestamps** are used for price age only, on an hours-to-days scale.

## 6. Questions for the auditors

- Can any sequence of `deposit`, `borrow`, `protect` and `liquidate` leave the Vault with HF < 1 positions that `liquidate` can't close?
- Is the `protect` closed form (`k = (T·D − P·u·L) / (P·(T(1−s) − L))`) safe under integer rounding at extreme values (tiny debt, huge collateral, target near trigger)?
- Is `CloakRegistry` safe against replaying a proof made for another token, LLTV or price, and against front-running `attest`?
- Can an `NightshiftAggregatorV3` consumer be misled by `roundId == updatedAt` semantics or by `getRoundData` for past rounds?

## 7. Before real assets (production checklist)

- Owner keys moved to a multisig (for example a Safe), with a timelock on `setMarket`, `setUpdater` and `setMaxMoveBps`.
- Two independent updaters, with an on-chain median or a deviation check against a second source.
- A pause on `borrow`. Repay and withdraw always stay open.
- Guardian sales routed to a DEX or an auction, not the Vault's own inventory.
- Bad-debt accounting, or Morpho Blue markets used directly, where it is handled.
- Re-run `scripts/lltv-study.mjs`. IBIT's 62.5% only clears its margin while Nightshift's overnight tracking is running (see `/docs#lltv`).
